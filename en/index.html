What changed

Patients ask questions, not keywords

Instead of typing "internal medicine Yongsan", patients ask an AI assistant for a clinic near a specific station that offers a specific service. The longer and more specific the question, the more the answer depends on a source the model trusts. A clinic becomes that source only when its own domain is readable, structured and consistent.

Readable

AI crawlers must receive the clinic name, address, departments and hours as text in the first response, not as a script that only people can render.

Structured

MedicalClinic, Physician, MedicalWebPage and FAQPage markup turn sentences into facts a model can confirm.

Consistent

Name, address, phone and hours must agree across the website, Naver Place, Google Maps and public health data.
